Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Smart Home is a private app used by one family. It is not offered to the public.

What it reads

With the family's permission, the app reads one Google account's calendars, read-only (the calendar.readonly scope). It reads calendar names, colors and event titles, times and locations. It cannot create, change or delete anything in Google.

How the data is used and stored

Events are copied to the family's own server so they can be shown on the family's screens. They are used for nothing else. The data is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or AI training.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnecting the account in the app deletes the stored events and withdraws the app's access at Google. Access can also be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
